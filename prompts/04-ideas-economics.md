# Step 4 — 20 ideas, revenue routes and costs

1. For each niche write 20 **substantially different** video ideas in
   `ideas` (title strings), grouped mentally into 3+ series. Base them on scan
   gaps, comment questions and keywords. `decide` flags near-duplicates;
   replace them. If you can't reach 20 distinct ideas, lower `content_depth`.
2. Fill `revenue_routes` with specific programs/sponsors/products and URLs.
3. Fill `economics`:
   - `monthly_views` low/mid/high — labelled scenarios, not forecasts.
   - `rpm_usd` low/mid/high and `rpm_source` (the user's own analytics if
     they have a channel in a similar niche; otherwise "assumption" plus any
     cited range). Never derive RPM from CPM. `vidiq_earnings_calculate` is a
     provider estimate — label it as such.
   - `external_revenue_usd` per case (0 if unknown).
   - `costs_usd` per month by line item (ElevenLabs, AI video/image tools,
     licences, editing time valued if the user wants), `videos_per_month`.
   - `basis`: "scenario" until pilot data exists, then "pilot".
